About Me

Welcome to my academic webpage. I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Academic Excellence, where I have been a faculty member since 2015. My research focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and their applications in real-world problem solving.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University in 2014, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. John Doe on neural network architectures for natural language processing. Prior to my current position, I was a postdoctoral researcher at MIT's Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL).

My work has been published in top-tier conferences and journals including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and Nature Machine Intelligence. I am passionate about both advancing the theoretical understanding of machine learning and developing practical applications that benefit society.

Research Interests

Deep Learning

Developing novel neural network architectures for improved performance and interpretability. Focus on attention mechanisms, transformer models, and their applications in various domains.

Natural Language Processing

Working on language understanding, generation, and multilingual models. Particular interest in low-resource languages and cross-lingual transfer learning.

AI Ethics and Fairness

Investigating bias in machine learning systems and developing methods for fair and responsible AI deployment in real-world applications.

Computer Vision

Exploring vision-language models and their applications in medical imaging, autonomous systems, and human-computer interaction.
