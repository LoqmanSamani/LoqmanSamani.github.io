About TorchDiff

A Python library for diffusion models

As a computational biologist with a passion for structural biology, I, Loghman Samani, embarked on a journey in 2025 to explore diffusion models—generative algorithms with the potential to revolutionize biology and medicine. These models can predict protein structures, design molecules, and unlock new insights into life sciences. To truly understand their power, I dove into their architecture, starting with a GitHub repository to review and implement original papers. After six months of dedicated work, I transformed this into TorchDiff, an open-source PyTorch library featuring DDPM, DDIM, SDE, and LDM, with unCLIP and Kandinsky on the horizon. TorchDiff is more than code; it’s a gateway for researchers to innovate at the intersection of AI and biology.
